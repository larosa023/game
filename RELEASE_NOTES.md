## What's new
- **The village has houses again.** Both houses in the starting village were missing their roofs and walls, along with many trees, crops, and market stalls. All of it is redrawn.
- **Bigger, resizable window.** The game opens at 1280×720, and the view and menus scale to whatever size you make the window.
- **Fullscreen:** press **F11** (or Alt+Enter), or use the option in Settings. The game remembers your choice.
- The FPS and debug text is hidden by default. Press **F3** to show it.
- New "Oasis" title logo and app icon.

Already on an older version? Click **Update** on the main menu.
