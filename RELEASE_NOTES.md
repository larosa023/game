## New area: the Mycelic Grove 🍄
A glowing violet portal has appeared in the meadow east of the village. Step up to it and press **E** to enter the **Mycelic Grove**, a mushroom village deep in a teal forest.

- **Explore:** a spore pond, four mushroom houses around a stone plaza, and tall-grass meadows.
- **Gather:** Crimson Caps, Verdant Fungus, and rare **Silver Spores** grow all over the Grove. Click one to pick it up. Something valuable is hidden in the far north-east corner.
- **Fight:** **Sporelings** are spore-infected roaches. They're tougher than the roaches in the basement, spit acid, and drop mushrooms when defeated.
- **Return home:** the portal you arrive at takes you back to the village.
